# MVP Scope

## MVP Goal

Prove that CLINICPILOTX can receive inbound inquiries, identify credible leads, notify the business owner, and keep a searchable lead record.

## Must Have

- Business/client setup.
- Lead inbox or inbound email capture.
- Email classification: credible lead, spam, unsure.
- Lead extraction: name, email, phone, requested service, message summary.
- Client notification by email.
- Dashboard list of leads.
- Manual review queue for unsure messages.

## Should Have

- Suggested reply draft.
- Basic status tracking: new, notified, replied, closed.
- Service/pricing FAQ knowledge per business.
- Simple activity log.

## Later

- SMS notifications.
- Auto-replies without human approval.
- Appointment booking integrations.
- CRM integrations.
- Multi-location support.
- HIPAA/BAA-ready infrastructure.

# CLINICPILOTX Product Spec

## One-Line Description

CLINICPILOTX is a lead automation and client intake assistant for clinics, med spas, dentists, salons, and similar service businesses.

## Target Users

- Plastic surgery clinics.
- Dental clinics and dental surgery offices.
- Medical spas.
- Beauty spas.
- Hair salons.
- Beauty parlors.
- Other appointment-based service businesses that receive inquiries by email, web form, chat, or social media.

## Core Problem

Service businesses receive messy inbound inquiries. Some are credible leads, some are spam, some are unclear, and staff often miss or delay responses. CLINICPILOTX should help sort, summarize, notify, and prepare replies so real leads get handled quickly.

## MVP Workflow

1. An inquiry arrives by email or form.
2. CLINICPILOTX classifies the message.
3. If credible, CLINICPILOTX extracts lead details and notifies the business owner.
4. If unsure, it goes to manual review.
5. If spam, it is ignored or logged.
6. The dashboard shows lead status and history.
7. AI-generated replies are drafts by default.

## MVP Data Fields

- Lead name.
- Email.
- Phone.
- Business/client account.
- Requested service.
- Message summary.
- Classification.
- Confidence.
- Urgency.
- Source.
- Status.
- Suggested reply.
- Created date.
- Last action.

## Initial Success Criteria

- Correctly identify credible leads in a small test set.
- Notify the business owner quickly.
- Keep a usable record of every credible or unsure inquiry.
- Let the owner demonstrate a real workflow to a potential paying client.

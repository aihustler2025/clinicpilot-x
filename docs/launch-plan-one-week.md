# One-Week MVP Launch Plan

## Goal

Ship a usable ClinicPilot X MVP for client demos and early users within one week.

## Priority Order

1. Audit the existing Lovable app.
2. Confirm backend and database setup.
3. Connect or export the code to GitHub.
4. Confirm the minimal client setup flow.
5. Build one real lead intake workflow.
6. Show real lead records in the dashboard.
7. Add owner notification by email.
8. Keep AI replies draft-only for MVP.

## Avoid For Week One

- Full SMS system unless a paying client needs it.
- Complex CRM integrations.
- Multi-location support.
- Full compliance architecture beyond basic careful data handling.
- Rebuilding the entire Lovable app without first auditing it.

## Demo Definition

A successful demo should show:

- A lead arrives from email or a test form.
- ClinicPilot X classifies the lead.
- The lead appears in the dashboard.
- The business owner receives a notification.
- A suggested reply draft is available.
- Unsure messages go to manual review.
